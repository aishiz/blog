- [ ] **Step 6: Write Часть 2 — Kimi Delta Attention, embed `AttentionCompare`**

Cross-link opening: same thesis already covered in [KV-кэш](/blog/kv-cache/) and [FlashAttention](/blog/flashattention/) — decode is memory-bound. FlashAttention attacked *how* the memory is accessed (IO-aware tiling) without changing what's stored; KDA attacks *how much* has to be stored in the first place, by replacing most attention layers with a linear-attention variant that keeps a fixed-size recurrent state instead of a growing KV-cache.

Mechanism, matching the paper's own terminology exactly (verified during planning): KDA extends Gated DeltaNet with a finer-grained gating mechanism for more effective use of the finite-state RNN memory; a bespoke chunkwise algorithm runs over a specialized variant of Diagonal-Plus-Low-Rank (DPLR) transition matrices for hardware efficiency. Layout: a 3:1 ratio of KDA layers to full-attention (Gated MLA) layers — most layers are cheap linear attention, every 4th layer keeps full attention for the modeling capacity linear attention alone can't match. A second, separately-published mechanism, Attention Residuals (arXiv 2603.15031, also Kimi Team) replaces standard fixed-weight residual accumulation with softmax attention over preceding layers' outputs — prevents the "diluted contribution" problem of very deep networks with plain residuals; K3 also uses the scalable "Block AttnRes" variant.
